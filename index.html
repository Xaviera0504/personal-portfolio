About

I grew up in Shanghai, built my early career in London, and now call New York home while I finish my Master's in Learning Analytics at Teachers College, Columbia University. Each city taught me something the last one couldn't — Shanghai gave me a head for scale and speed, London taught me to ask why before what, and New York keeps pushing me to make the story land in the room.

Working across three cultures didn't just change my passport stamps — it changed how I read data. The same attrition number means something different to a manager in Shanghai than it does to one in London, and a dashboard is only as useful as the trust you've built before you present it. That's the lens I bring to people analytics: rigorous with the numbers, genuinely curious about the humans behind them.
